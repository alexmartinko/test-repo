## Budget strategies (recomputed on FantasyPros 2026 values)

Ranked best-fit for this league. Bench trimmed vs. the old sheets — in an

| Bench pool | ~17 | ~12 | ~13 | ~14 | ~13 |

1. **Studs** — Plan A. 3 alphas (~$166 across RB1 + WR1 + Flex-1), rest $1–4 +
   darts. Shallow league + ceiling-rewarding scoring = concentrate.
2. **RB-heavy** — the pivot. Top-3 RB + strong RB2, punt QB/TE/WR2. Steepest
   talent cliff + best bonus equity is at RB. Most of Studs' upside, better
   floor.
3. **Balanced** — the bailout if the top gets torched and concentration isn't
   available at a price. Robust, leaves EV on the table.
4. **Elite QB** — "Josh Allen or bust." Only QB the bonuses justify a premium
   for (pass *and* rush). Skip unless Allen falls to ~$25 — QB is deep in
   8-team and the same bonuses lift the $9–10 dual-threats.
5. **Elite TE** — only if McBride (~$35) / Bowers (~$30) drops. Premium at the
   position the bonuses barely touch, in a format shallow enough to stream TE.

Decision tree at the table: **Studs → RB-heavy → Balanced**; Elite QB/TE are
opportunistic only.

## Bubble-case notes (web, Sept 1 2026)

- **Joe Burrow** — FantasyPros $4 is a genuine misprice; consensus top-3 QB,
  4,500-yд / 35-TD ceiling, ideal for the passing-yardage bonuses. Availability
  risk (7+ games missed in 2 of last 3 seasons). App tags him T2 with a note.
- **Malik Nabers** — was "out till October," now a possible Wk 1 with a
  ramp-up; still a top-15 WR. Sheet [INJ] overstates the fade.
- **Jeremiyah Love** — high-ankle sprain, "hopeful" for Wk 1 but tricky.
  Explains FP $21 vs ESPN $45. Bid closer to FP with upside.
- **Breece Hall** — groin, 2–3 weeks, expected back for opener. Small tax.
- **Emeka Egbuka** — turf toe, may miss Wk 1, can linger. Real risk at $17.
- **Puka Nacua** — psoas + minor groin, expected Wk 1; could repeat as overall
  WR1. Keep at the top of T1.
- **Kenneth Walker III** — camp riser, workhorse talk in KC, but "risky"
  profile. Fair at T4.
